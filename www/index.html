Why Gabija?

Rethinking family management for the privacy-conscious era

Family-Centric Design

In today's digital world, managing a household shouldn't mean compromising your family's privacy. Gabija is built from the ground up to serve modern families who want to stay organized without handing their sensitive data to third-party services.

Privacy First, Always

Unlike cloud-based alternatives, Gabija runs entirely on your own infrastructure. Your financial data, family schedules, and personal information stay private and secure in your home or on your server.

Built for Modern Life

From managing household expenses to coordinating family schedules, Gabija brings together essential tools in one cohesive platform. No more juggling multiple apps that don't work together.

Self-hosted architecture means your data never leaves your control. Advanced encryption keeps your family's information secure.

Extend functionality with a powerful plugin system. WASM-based sandboxing ensures third-party extensions run safely.
